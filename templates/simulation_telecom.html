How to Interpret the Simulation

The simulation demonstrates how the trained machine-learning model responds when the customer's input conditions are changed.

A negative probability change means the simulated customer state received a lower predicted churn probability from the model.

A positive probability change means the simulated customer state received a higher predicted churn probability from the model.

This is a model-based scenario analysis. It does not prove that changing these conditions will produce the same outcome in the real world.
